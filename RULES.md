# Rules · AI brain for industry

These rules govern the hackathon "AI brain for industry", held on Saturday 14 November 2026. Applying to the hackathon means accepting them.

## 1. Organiser

The hackathon is organised by Kakashi Venture Accelerator Srl (KVA), Via Sant'Antonino 17B, 10139 Turin, Italy, which can be contacted at [hello@kakashi.ventures](mailto:hello@kakashi.ventures).

## 2. Nature of the event

The hackathon is free to enter. It is a contest of ideas and projects in applied AI for industry and, under art. 6, paragraph 1, letter a) of Italian Presidential Decree 430/2001, it is not a prize competition or prize operation. The award is either consideration for the winning project, which the team assigns to KVA, or an investment by KVA in a company built on the project, as the team chooses under section 10.

## 3. Who can take part

Participants must be 18 or older on 14 November 2026. KVA staff, jury members and their relatives can join as mentors but cannot receive the award. Each person can belong to one team only.

## 4. Applications and places

Applications are made on [kakashi.ventures/hackathon](https://www.kakashi.ventures/en/hackathon#signup), Eventbrite or Luma, and close on Friday 13 November 2026 at 12:00 (CET). There are 25 places in Turin and 30 online. KVA reads every application and selects participants for a mix of skills; its choice is final.

A person takes part only once KVA has confirmed their place by email. Confirmed participants sign in to the participants' dashboard within 7 days of that email, or they may be excluded. Those taking part in Turin confirm their attendance in the dashboard by Saturday 7 November 2026, one week before the event, or they may be excluded from future KVA hackathons. Anyone who cannot attend tells KVA as soon as possible, so that the place can be offered to someone else.

## 5. Teams

A team has between 1 and 5 members. People can apply as a team, on their own or looking for a team, but every team member must apply and be confirmed. People without a team can join one at team matching; KVA helps, but assigns no one to a team against their will. Teams are fixed at the start of phase I (10:30) and cannot change after that, except in exceptional circumstances.

## 6. Format

The hackathon takes place on-site at KVA in Turin and online, at the times listed in the [agenda](README.md#agenda), which are in CET. Online participants follow the talks and present over a video call, whose link KVA sends to confirmed participants. On-site and online teams follow the same rules, compete for the same award and are judged by the same jury.

## 7. What teams build

Projects are mainly built during the event. Teams can bring an idea, a dataset they have the right to use, and open-source libraries, frameworks and models. They can use AI tools to write code, text and images and inside their solution, and state in their submission which models and AI tools they used. The free credits and API keys provided by KVA are for the hackathon only and cannot be shared or used for anything else.

## 8. Checkpoints and submission

Before the first and final show-offs, every team sends the material listed in KVA's instructions. Teams that miss a checkpoint or the submission can be left out of the evaluation.

## 9. Jury and evaluation

KVA appoints the jury and publishes its members in this repository before the event. A jury member with a personal or work tie to a team does not score that team. The jury scores each project from 1 to 5 on each of the following criteria:

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Fit with the challenge: memory, ontologies or AI brains | 25% |
| Technical quality of what works on the day | 25% |
| Real problem and impact for an industry | 25% |
| Business case, as checked at the financial validation | 20% |
| Clarity of the demo | 5% |

In a tie, the project with the higher score on fit with the challenge wins. The jury may decide not to make the award if no project reaches a sufficient level. Its decision is final.

## 10. Award

The winning team is announced at 18:30 and chooses one of two options. All team members make the choice together, in writing to [hello@kakashi.ventures](mailto:hello@kakashi.ventures), within 14 days of the event.

**Option A.** KVA pays the team €1,500 in exchange for the project. By accepting this rules, every team member assigns to KVA all economic rights to the project, including its code, documentation and designs, with no limits of time, territory or use. KVA can then develop, use, license or sell the project and build a company on it, with no further payment to the team. Open-source components and third-party data in the project stay under their own licences, and the authors keep their moral rights and can show the project in their portfolio. KVA pays within 60 days of the signed agreement, to the team members in equal shares unless they agree otherwise in writing, and withholds any tax required by law.

**Option B.** The team incorporates a company to develop the project within 90 days of the event and brings the project into it. KVA invests €1,500 in the company, for 15% of its share capital, and supports it with its venture studio through the initial acceleration phase. The terms are set out in a written agreement between KVA and the founders, discussed in two weeks after the hackathon. If no answer is obtained from the team in 14 days, the award lapses.

## 11. Ownership and licences

Each project belongs to the team that builds it. The team chooses its licence and is required to publish its code in a public repository. KVA acquires no ownership of the project and no licence to use it, except as set out in this section and in section 10.

For 12 months after the event, KVA has a right of first refusal on every team's project. Before a team sells, assigns or grants an exclusive licence to the project, or transfers control of a company that holds it, the team offers the same deal to KVA in writing, together with any third-party offer. KVA has 30 days to accept on the same terms. If KVA declines or does not answer, the team can close the deal with the third party within 90 days, on terms no more favourable to the third party than those offered to KVA. After 90 days, or if the terms change in the third party's favour, the deal is offered to KVA again.

For the same 12 months, a team that raises money for a company built on the project informs KVA before the round closes. KVA then has 15 days to take part in the round, on the same terms as the other investors. This does not block the round or change its terms.

For the winning team, the option chosen under section 10 replaces these rights: under option A the project passes to KVA, and under option B bringing the project into the company does not trigger them and, from then on, the company's bylaws and the agreement in section 10 apply. These rights do not limit a team's own use of the project, open-source licences or the publication of its code.

Each team confirms that its work is original and that it has the right to use everything in it, and holds KVA harmless from any claim by third parties over the project. KVA may use team names, project titles, descriptions, and photos and video of the presentations and demos to promote the hackathon and its own activities, in any medium; images of people are used as set out in section 14. Anything shared during the event, including in presentations, is not confidential, so teams protect what they need to before sharing it. Data and materials provided by KVA are for the hackathon only, unless they come with their own licence.

## 12. Conduct

Participants treat everyone with respect, in Turin and online. Harassment, discrimination, cheating, plagiarism or getting in the way of other teams lead to exclusion, and KVA can exclude a person or a team that breaks these rules at any time.

## 13. Responsibility

Participants are responsible for their laptops and belongings. KVA is not responsible for loss, theft or damage, or for failures of the internet or of platforms and services run by others.

## 14. Privacy, photos and video

KVA processes personal data as described in its [privacy notice](https://www.kakashi.ventures/en/privacy). Photos and video are taken at the event. In the application form, each participant chooses whether KVA may use images of them, and can change that choice by writing to [hello@kakashi.ventures](mailto:hello@kakashi.ventures).

## 15. Changes

KVA can change the date, venue, agenda or these rules only for reasons outside its control. Every change is published in this repository and communicated to confirmed participants by email, and anyone for whom a change does not work can withdraw.

## 16. Law

These rules are governed by Italian law, and the court of Turin has jurisdiction.
