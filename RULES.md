# Rules · AI brain for industry

These rules apply to the hackathon "AI brain for industry" on Saturday 14 November 2026.
By applying you accept them.

## 1. Organiser

Kakashi Venture Accelerator Srl (KVA), Via Sant'Antonino 17B, 10139 Turin, Italy.
Contact: [hello@kakashi.ventures](mailto:hello@kakashi.ventures).

## 2. Nature of the event

The hackathon is free to enter.
It is a contest of ideas and projects in applied AI.
Under art. 6, paragraph 1, letter a) of Italian Presidential Decree 430/2001, it is not a prize competition or prize operation.
The prize recognises the merit of the winning project.

## 3. Who can take part

- You must be 18 or older on 14 November 2026.
- KVA staff, jury members and their relatives can join as mentors but cannot win the prize.
- Each person can be in one team only.

## 4. Applications and places

- Apply on [kakashi.ventures/hackathon](https://www.kakashi.ventures/en/hackathon#signup), Eventbrite or Luma.
- Applications close on Friday 13 November 2026 at 12:00 (CET).
- There are 25 places in Turin and 30 places online.
- We read every application and choose participants for a mix of skills. Our choice is final.
- You take part only after we confirm your place by email.
- Sign in to the participants' dashboard within 7 days of that email, or you may be excluded.
- If you take part in Turin, confirm your attendance in the dashboard by Saturday 7 November 2026, one week before the event, or you may be excluded from future KVA hackathons.
- If you cannot come, tell us as soon as possible so we can offer your place to someone else.

## 5. Teams

- A team has 1 to 5 people.
- You can apply with your team, on your own or looking for a team.
- Every team member must apply and be confirmed.
- People without a team can join one at team matching. We help, but we do not force anyone into a team.
- A team cannot mix people in Turin and online.
- Teams are fixed at the start of phase I (10:30). After that, no changes are possible, except for exceptional circumstances.

## 6. Format

- On-site at KVA in Turin, and online. The times are in the [agenda](README.md#agenda) and are in CET.
- Online participants follow the talks and present over a video call. We send the link to confirmed participants.
- On-site and online teams follow the same rules, compete for the same prize and are judged by the same jury.

## 7. What you build

- You build during the event. Code written before 14 November does not count towards the evaluation.
- You can bring an idea, a dataset you have the right to use, and open-source libraries, frameworks and models.
- You can use AI tools to write code, text and images, and inside your solution.
- Say which models and AI tools you used in your submission.
- The free credits and API keys we give you are for the hackathon only. Do not share them or use them for anything else.

## 8. Checkpoints and submission

- Before the first and final show-offs, every team has to send the material specified by the given instructions.
- Teams that miss a checkpoint or the submission can be left out of the evaluation.

## 9. Jury and evaluation

- KVA appoints the jury and publishes its members in this repository before the event.
- A jury member with a personal or work tie to a team does not score that team.
- The jury scores each project from 1 to 5 on each criterion:

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Fit with the challenge: memory, ontologies or AI brains | 25% |
| Technical quality of what works on the day | 25% |
| Real problem and impact for an industry | 25% |
| Business case, as checked at the financial validation | 20% |
| Clarity of the demo | 5% |

- In a tie, the project with the higher score on fit with the challenge wins.
- The jury may decide not to award the prize if no project reaches a sufficient level.
- The jury's decision is final.

## 10. Prize

- €1,500 goes to the winning team. We announce it at 18:30.
- The prize is split equally among the team members, unless the team tells us another split in writing.
- We pay by bank transfer to each member within 30 days of the event, once we have the details we need. Taxes apply as required by Italian law.
- The prize cannot be exchanged for anything else.

## 11. Ownership and licences

- What you build belongs to you and your team. KVA does not take ownership of it and does not get a licence to use it, except as set out in this section.
- You choose the licence of your project. You do not have to publish your code.
- For 12 months after the event, KVA has a right of first refusal on every team's project:
  - Before your team sells the project, assigns it or grants an exclusive licence to it, you offer the same deal to KVA first.
  - Send KVA the terms in writing, with the third party's offer if there is one. KVA has 30 days to accept on the same terms.
  - If KVA declines or does not answer, you can close the deal with the third party within 90 days, on terms no better for them than those offered to KVA. After 90 days, or if the terms change in their favour, you offer it to KVA again.
- For the same 12 months, if your team raises money for a company built on the project, tell KVA before the round closes. KVA has 15 days to say whether it wants to invest in that round, on the same terms as the other investors. This does not block the round or change its terms.
- These rights do not limit your own use of the project, open-source licences or publishing your code.
- You confirm that your work is original and that you have the right to use everything in it. If someone else claims rights over your project, that is your responsibility.
- KVA may show your team name, the project title, a short description and photos of the demo to tell people about the hackathon.
- Anything you share during the event, including in the presentations, is not confidential. Protect what you need to protect before you share it.
- Data or materials KVA provides are for the hackathon only, unless they come with their own licence.

## 12. Conduct

- Be respectful to everyone, in Turin and online.
- Harassment, discrimination, cheating, plagiarism or getting in the way of other teams lead to exclusion.
- KVA can exclude a person or a team that breaks these rules, at any time.

## 13. Responsibility

- You are responsible for your laptop and your belongings.
- KVA is not responsible for loss, theft or damage, or for failures of the internet, platforms and services run by others.

## 14. Privacy, photos and video

- KVA processes your personal data as described in the [privacy notice](https://www.kakashi.ventures/en/privacy).
- We take photos and video at the event. In the application form you choose whether we may use images of you. You can change your mind by writing to [hello@kakashi.ventures](mailto:hello@kakashi.ventures).

## 15. Changes

- KVA can change the date, venue, agenda or these rules only for reasons outside its control.
- We publish every change in this repository and tell confirmed participants by email.
- If a change does not work for you, you can withdraw.

## 16. Law

These rules are governed by Italian law. The court of Turin has jurisdiction.
