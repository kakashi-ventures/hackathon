## The challenge

**The AI brain for industry.** Pick a real problem in applied AI and solve it on your own or in a
team, with ontologies, shared memory or custom-built AI brains.

The questions the challenge asks (use them to test R4 · Challenge fit):

*Memory & AI*
- How does a memory tell when a rule has been superseded?
- How do many agents write to the same memory without contradicting each other?
- How do you record why a decision was made?

*Custom ontologies for AI* — an ontology is the formal specification of a shared conceptualisation:
the classes, relations and axioms of a domain.
- How do you build an ontology?
- How do you update it when the company changes the way it works?
- How does an agent use it to reason, and not only to search?

An idea scores R4 ≥ 4 only if its core flow answers one of these questions with a working mechanism.
The technology map in `moat.md` §4 is organised around them.

Profiles the organisers are looking for: researchers, deep-tech entrepreneurs, people with vertical
expertise in one sector, curious and creative people with an idea to build. You do not need to code:
a team also needs people who do not.
